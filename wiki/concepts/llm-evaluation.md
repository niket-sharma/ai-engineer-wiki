# LLM Evaluation

## TL;DR
Methods for measuring LLM quality at scale, from task benchmarks to LLM-as-judge.

## Intuition
Evaluating LLMs is hard because 'correct' is often subjective. The field has converged on three main approaches: standardized benchmarks (MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEval), human preference collection, and using another LLM as an automated judge.
